BuddyNow Cookie and Local Storage Notice

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This notice explains how BuddyNow uses cookies, local storage, and secure device storage.

1. Mobile App

The BuddyNow mobile app does not use browser cookies. It uses limited on-device storage so the app can work normally.

StoragePurposeWhat is stored
AsyncStorageRemember app preferencesLanguage preference and similar non-sensitive settings
iOS Keychain through SecureStoreKeep you signed in securelySession token for the 30-day login session

2. Website

The public BuddyNow legal and landing pages are static pages. We do not intentionally set analytics cookies, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking tools.

These pages keep one item in your browser's local storage: the language you pick with the EN / 简 / 繁 switch, so the next page you open stays in that language. It is not an identifier, it is never sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting and DNS infrastructure, such as GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, may process standard technical request information needed to deliver the website and protect the service. BuddyNow does not use this for behavioural advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Crash Reporting

BuddyNow does not use third-party analytics, ad networks, behavioural tracking SDKs, Facebook SDK, or Google Analytics. We do use Sentry for crash and error reporting on the backend and mobile app — see the Privacy Policy § 6 for what Sentry receives and how it is constrained (no email, no message bodies, no location; by-design business errors are filtered out).

4. Your Choices

  • You can delete the app to remove app-local preference storage from your device.
  • You can sign out or delete your account to revoke server sessions.
  • You can clear the saved language choice, and any cookies infrastructure may set in future, through your browser settings.

5. Contact

Questions can be sent to support@astralogy.org.

本说明解释 BuddyNow 如何使用 Cookie、本地存储和安全设备存储。

1. 移动 App

BuddyNow 移动 App 不使用浏览器 Cookie。App 仅使用有限的设备本地存储，以保证正常运行。

存储方式用途存储内容
AsyncStorage记住 App 偏好语言偏好及类似的非敏感设置
通过 SecureStore 使用 iOS Keychain安全地保持登录状态30 天登录会话使用的会话令牌

2. 网站

BuddyNow 的公开法律页面和落地页是静态页面。我们不会主动设置分析 Cookie、广告 Cookie、追踪像素或跨站追踪工具。

这些页面会在你的浏览器本地存储里保存一项内容：你用 EN / 简 / 繁 切换选择的语言，以便你打开下一页时保持同一语言。它不是标识符，不会发送给我们，清除浏览器数据即可移除。

GitHub Pages、Cloudflare 等托管和 DNS 基础设施可能会处理提供网站和保护服务所需的标准技术请求信息。BuddyNow 不会将这些信息用于行为广告或跨 App 追踪。

BuddyNow 不使用第三方分析、广告网络、行为追踪 SDK、Facebook SDK 或 Google Analytics。我们在后端和移动 App 上使用 Sentry 进行崩溃和错误上报——详见隐私政策第 6 节了解 Sentry 接收什么以及如何受限（不发送邮箱、消息内容、位置；业务正常错误会被过滤掉）。

  • 你可以删除 App，以移除设备上的 App 本地偏好存储。
  • 你可以退出登录或删除账号，以撤销服务端会话。
  • 你可以通过浏览器设置清除已保存的语言选择，以及未来基础设施可能设置的任何 Cookie。

5. 联系方式

如有问题，请发送至 support@astralogy.org。
