About

Astralogy is a software company. We believe what becomes most scarce in the AI era is genuine connection between people, and the warmth of human presence. What is most worth creating is richer journeys of the spirit and more varied experiences of life.

What we believe

In an era when AI generates more content than humans can recognize or consume, what becomes scarce and precious is real connection between people. A moment of being truly heard. An activity or place you've long wanted to step into but never had the courage or chance. An afternoon of play worth remembering.

The software we make exists to support these — to help people more easily take one more step toward a fuller, richer life. Software that heals rather than hooks.

One product at a time. Made with warmth.

01

Healing, not addictive

Our products leave you better than they found you. No engagement metrics that depend on making you feel worse. We ease anxiety; we don't sell it.

02

Real connection

We design for real relationships between real people — not the one-way watching of deceptively-perfect virtual content, not the virtual companionship simulated by algorithms.

03

Immersive experience
